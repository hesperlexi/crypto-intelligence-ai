This page explains what Crypto Intelligence AI stores about you and why. We do not sell your data.

## Sign-in

You sign in with your Google account (Firebase Authentication). We receive your name, email address and a user ID
from Google. We never see your Google password.

## What we store (Google Cloud Firestore)

- **Account and membership:** email, name, user ID, membership status, plan and expiry date, and its history.
- **Payments:** the payment method you chose, plan, amount, and the payment screenshot and/or order / transaction ID
  you submit, so the owner can check it.
- **Activity:** when you last used the app, an approximate country worked out from your device's time zone (or
  language setting), your time zone, and the type of device (for example "Android app" or "Desktop browser"). No GPS
  or location permission is used.
- **Notifications:** if you turn on notifications, a push token for your device with the browser description, and the
  last notification error (to help fix delivery problems).
- **Your settings and use of the app:** practice-trading accounts and trades (virtual money), auto-trade settings,
  alert rules, coins you added and when you last read your alerts.

## Data your browser fetches from others

- Live prices and charts are loaded by your browser directly from Binance (and its public data host). Binance sees
  your IP address as with any website.
- Some charts are embedded from TradingView, coin icons come from a public CDN (jsDelivr), and the owner's user list
  shows country flags from flagcdn.com.
- Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

## Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, which records page views and basic usage (such as device type and
approximate region) to help us understand how the app is used.

## Who can see it

Your data is protected by sign-in and database rules: you can read your own data, and the owner of the app can see
member records to approve payments and give support. The app's automated engine reads what it needs to run alerts and
auto-trade.

## Your choices

You can turn notifications off in Settings at any time. To ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion, email
support@cryptointelai.com.

## Questions

Details on data retention, the responsible company and your rights where you live: please contact
support@cryptointelai.com.
